Architectural (heavy timber): 4× members per NDS Supplement Table 4A/4B (dimension lumber, size factor CF), 5×5 and larger per Table 4D (Beams & Stringers / Posts & Timbers, CF=(12/d)1/9 for d > 12″), dressed sizes per NDS Table 1B; wet-service CM per table footnotes; connections by the Engineer of Record (simple bolt yield-limit check only). Geometry, takeoff and member check are for budgeting/conceptual layout. Final truss design, member sizes, plates, bracing and uplift connections are by the truss manufacturer (sealed truss design drawings per IRC R802.10 / IBC 2303.4) and the Engineer of Record. Reference design values per NDS Supplement 2018 Table 4A/4B (SYP 4B). Analysis is simplified: top-chord heel compression from joint equilibrium, panel bending w·L²/10, in-plane buckling le=0.8×panel length (continuous chord), sheathing braces weak axis. Wind auto value: qh=0.00256·Kz(B 0.57 / C 0.85 / D 1.03 at h ≤ 15 ft)·Kd(0.85)·V², net uplift coeff. ≈1.18 (interior zone, enclosed) – verify per ASCE 7-22 Ch. 30.
